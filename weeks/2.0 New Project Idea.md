## 01 — Project Overview

I want to create a generative intro animation for my personal portfolio using p5.js.

The animation will introduce my name, **Phariya Heng**, through organic shapes, particle movement and experimental typography.

My goal is to create something that feels natural, fluid and alive rather than geometric or mechanical.

I want to explore how organic elements can move independently, interact with a particle system and eventually come together to form typography.

The animation will serve as the opening experience of my portfolio.
