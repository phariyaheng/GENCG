The idea was to transform my existing organic, Perlin Noise-based drawing into a temporary representation of my name, «Phariya Heng». The typography should emerge naturally from the structure rather than appear as a separate graphic element.

## **Experimenting with AI**

I used AI to visualise my idea and test whether it understood my design intention. Through several iterations, I explored:

- Organic letterforms with varying sizes, positions and densities.
- Partially hidden and fragmented letters.
- The integration of small and large bubbles into the typography.
- Different levels of abstraction and readability.

## **Visual Exploration**

The generated images show different interpretations of my idea. I also experimented with storyboards to explore how the structure could grow from the centre, gradually form my name and quickly contract again.

## **Reflection**

AI understood the general concept but struggled to achieve the desired balance between abstraction and readability. The letters often appeared too uniform, too clearly defined or almost completely disappeared into the organic structure.![[Bildschirmfoto 2026-11-24 um 15.52.47.png]]
