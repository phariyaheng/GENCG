# **New Animation Concept – Growth, Identity & Flash**

## **Concept**

My new idea is to create a dynamic transition from the organic drawing to my portfolio. The animation retains the original Perlin Noise aesthetic but introduces a more expressive sequence of movements.

## **Animation Sequence**

1. **Growth** – The organic elements emerge from the centre of the screen and gradually expand outwards. Small and large bubbles grow, while flowing lines connect them.
2. **Identity** – During the expansion, my name, «Phariya Heng», gradually emerges from the organic structure. Some letters remain recognisable, while others blend into the surrounding elements.
3. **Contraction** – As soon as the structure reaches the edge of the screen, everything rapidly contracts towards the centre.
4. **Flash** – A brief flash of light marks the final moment of contraction.
5. **Reveal** – The flash creates a seamless transition into my portfolio website.

# **Feedback – 01.12**

The overall concept received positive feedback. The idea was considered interesting and useful for the portfolio.
## **Suggestions for Improvement**

- **Colour:** Introduce colour into the organic structure and explore how it could enhance the visual experience.
- **Depth:** Experiment with three-dimensional effects to make the organic elements appear more spatial and immersive.
